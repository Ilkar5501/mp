# v12.1.1 — Duo searches and emoji

Apply this patch over v12.1.0. Stop the bot, replace the supplied files and restart.
Keep your existing database and environment settings. No database changes are needed.

Collection and gallery name searches for Oikawa or Kageyama include the Duo.
Exact variant/title searches remain specific. Duo collection/gallery rows use
`<:duo:1570428970460250212>`. Card and gallery detail views use the emoji's image
as the header icon next to the character name, replacing the plain Duo heading.

Ownership behavior is unchanged and verified: Young Emperor's extra allowance,
King of the Lost Court's duplication chance and the Duo's currency bonus use
the highest-merged currently owned copy. Fusion, transfer, recycling or donation
of the last copy stops that card's future collection bonuses. Another retained
copy continues at its own merge level. Earned rewards and used cap safeguards
remain; transferring a card out and back does not refresh an allowance.
