# Clan Contest: the revised 14 formations

One formation appears per day. The existing two-week shuffle is retained. Featured units are +10, Neutral and unequipped; six inactive supports grant only the slots shown below. School bonds still apply. Nozawa is a support in this rotation; both Korai positions have separate formations.

Only the featured unit receives random ally grants. Supports cannot act, enter combat, be targeted or trigger Ultimates. Front/back labels are the starting positions; explicitly named positional exceptions follow the contest rules. Visible stat bonuses use the normal highest-value rule, so duplicate bonuses do not add together.

## Slot restrictions that affect team building

- Oikawa supports only Ushijima, front-line Korai and Tenma. His own featured formation uses his full personal kit.
- Atsumu is C-only as a support: there is no Excellence damage from him.
- Kenma is B/C-only as a support: there is no Fatigue generation from him. Yamamoto is therefore a C-slot support for Kuroo rather than a featured Fatigue attacker.
- Usuri is C-only as a support: there is no Siphon or B-slot Pow/Jmp aura.
- Last Hope Kiryu is C-only as a support and ignores Burden. His useful reaction here is the enemy-crit debuff.
- Shirabu is C-only as a support. His maximum-cooldown designation is an A skill and does not apply.
- Prodigious Talent C cannot choose the same active ally on consecutive turns. On the intervening turn it uses its own fallback; this does not buff the featured unit.
- The formations use the allowed slots and current engine rules. They are distinct challenges, not a claim of mathematically optimal teams.
