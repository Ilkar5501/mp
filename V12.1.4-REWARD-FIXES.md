# v12.1.4 — reward fixes

- Tournament champion reward: 300 Coins per daily allowance. Independently,
  tournaments with at least four human entrants pay 150 Coins to every human
  entrant, including losers. A qualifying champion receives both. Each component
  banks within the week and has one extra discounted allowance with Young Emperor.
  A +10 Young Emperor therefore allows 300 + 150 win Coins and 150 + 75 human
  participation Coins per daily allowance. CPUs do not receive currency.
- Oikawa's extra fresh-team Gauntlet clear includes the 50-Coin new-lineup bonus
  before applying merge × 5%. At +10 that is (200 + 50) × 50% = 125 Coins.
  Repeated weekly lineups have no fresh-team bonus and pay 100 on that extra clear.
- Battles, Raids, Tiny Titans, AF1 and tournaments show exhausted allowances for
  eligible wins and successful Furious Upstart loss rolls. PvP now displays the
  loser's Hinata reward or the reason it could not pay. Actual records/Elo remain.
- Kageyama duplication still requires an eligible original drop; duplicates do
  not consume the original drop cap. Currency/AF1 Marks are not duplicated.
- Tournament quest progress shows win and human-participation components
  separately. Existing paid combined-ledger caps are migrated without changing
  wallets. Historical partial payments cannot be reconstructed perfectly after
  replay expiry; the migration conservatively preserves existing extra-use flags.

Back up the database, install the update and restart. No new dependencies.
