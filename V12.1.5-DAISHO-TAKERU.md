# v12.1.5 — Daisho, Takeru and Zunda Mochi Bento

- Daisho's Venomous Followthrough has no Speed requirement. An Ultimate still
  adds 15 damage. It grants a follow-up for that combat only if the foe has a
  visible stat penalty when the Ultimate triggers. Negative statuses alone do
  not qualify. Once granted, removing the penalty does not revoke the follow-up;
  normal follow-up denial still applies.
- Takeru's Flow State keeps the original Spd ≥ foe Spd−4 condition in every
  trigger/bonus path. Rally Surge restores 20 HP after an Ultimate-trigger combat.
- Zunda Mochi Bento is exclusive to Takeru — Flashing Salmon. It reduces maximum
  Ultimate cooldown by 1 once at match start, grants Qik/Spd+5 during combat, and
  grants Inspiration to the unit and allies through their next action whenever
  its Ultimate triggers. The match-start reduction does not accumulate across
  separate battles or mutate the collection card's base cooldown.
- Inspiration halves each non-Ultimate percentage damage-reduction value, from
  character skills, equipment or named statuses: 40% becomes 20%, not −10%.
  It also applies to the triggering attack if granted before damage calculation.
  Ultimate percentage reduction, flat reduction, barriers and fixed-damage
  protection are unchanged. Multiple Inspiration applications refresh the
  status instead of stacking another reduction multiplier. Equipment-granted
  Inspiration is not counted as an allied character skill by Bokuto/the Duo.
- Takeru's Challenge is appended to Tiny Titans so existing challenge indexes
  remain stable. It uses the normal 50% shard roll, an earned allowance of 25
  Bento shards per day banked within the week, and normal duplication safeguards.
  Craft: 25 Bento shards + 25 Takeru shards + 500 Coins. Recycle: 25 Clan Dollars.

Restart to load the updated definitions and combat hooks. No database migration
or new dependency is required. The cumulative ZIP includes all previous updates
and SQLite retention. Preserve the existing database and environment settings.
